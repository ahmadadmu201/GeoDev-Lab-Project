GIS-BASED ANALYSIS OF HEALTH FACILITY DISTRIBUTION AND POPULATION COVERAGE BIRNIN KEBBI LGA, KEBBI STATE.
How well does the spatial distribution of health facilities correspond with population distribution in Birnin Kebbi, Kebbi State?
why it matters?
1. to know where health facilities are located and where the population is concentrated
2. to identify areas where the population may have limited access to health facilities
3. to help understand whether health facilities are adequately distributed in relation to the population
4. to provide useful spatial information that can support better planning and allocation of health facilities.
DATA NEEDED;
1. health facility locations 
2. population estimates
3. ward boundaries
4. LGA boundaries
5. road network
6. settlement
