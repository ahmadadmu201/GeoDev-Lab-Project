GIS-BASED ASSESSMENT OF THE SPATIAL DISTRIBUTION AND ACCESSIBILITY OF HEALTH FACILITIES IN BIRNIN KEBBI LGA, KEBBI SATE.
why it matters?
1. To identify health facilities that are difficult to reach because of poor road connectivity
2. To identify areas with limited proximity to healthcare facilities based on distance from existing health centres. 
3. To provide a clear map that can support evidence-based decision-making

DATABASE;
1. Health facility locations 
2. Ward boundaries
4. LGA boundaries
5. Road network
